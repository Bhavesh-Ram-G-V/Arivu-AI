Arivu AI (அறிவு AI)

A practical, privacy-first web navigator connecting citizens of Tamil Nadu to 100% free government-run sports, skill development, libraries, and cultural centers.

🧭 Overview

Navigating public resources shouldn't require jumping through hoops or filling out invasive forms. Arivu AI provides deterministic, rule-based guidance that maps citizen needs directly to verified district institutional pathways across Tamil Nadu.

✨ Key Features

Strictly Free Resources: Excludes healthcare, finance, and jobs to focus entirely on free government training, coaching, and public facilities.

Actionable Guidance: Every query returns clear, structured instructions:

Where to go (Exact district office/complex)

Whom to meet (Designated officer)

What to carry (Required verification documents)

Privacy First: Zero data collection. No Aadhaar numbers, phone numbers, roll numbers, or bank details are ever asked for or stored.

Desktop-First UI: Built with a clean, responsive widescreen layout using React and Tailwind CSS, featuring soft terracotta/cream aesthetics and crisp SVG iconography.

🏛️ Institutional Pathways Mapped

Sports & Fitness: District Sports Complex (SDAT) -> District Sports Officer (DSO)

Skills & Technology: District Skill Training Office / RSETI (TNSDC) -> Assistant Director

Libraries & Study: District Central Library / Branch Library -> District Librarian

Folk Art & Music: District Government Music School -> Headmaster

🛠️ Tech Stack

Frontend: React (JSX)

Styling: Tailwind CSS (Custom "Elderly-Guide" Terracotta & Cream Palette)

Icons: Inline SVG Lucide-style icons

Audio: Browser Speech Synthesis (ta-IN) with native rate and punctuation formatting
